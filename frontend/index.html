Research preview — not a medical device

This demonstration uses synthetic patient records and a baseline machine-learning model. It is intended for education, portfolio demonstration and research exploration only. It has not been clinically validated, does not diagnose or predict an individual's actual health outcome, and must not be used to guide treatment. The biological network combines dataset associations with curated context; it does not prove causation.
